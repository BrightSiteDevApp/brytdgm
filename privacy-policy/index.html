Privacy Policy

Last revised: 24 October 2026

BRYT DGM respects your privacy and is committed to responsible handling of personal information. Please read this policy to understand what information we collect, how we use it, when it may be shared and the choices available to you.

1. Who We Are

BRYT Digital Market ("BRYT DGM", "we", "us" or "our") is an online marketplace operated by BRYT Digital Services, a business name registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), registration number BN 9944107. BRYT Technologies is a brand associated with our digital products. For information processed in connection with the marketplace, BRYT Digital Services is the responsible platform operator.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, register an account, list products, communicate with vendors or use other marketplace features.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use BRYT DGM, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact details: name, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number and other information you choose to provide.
  • Account information: username, login identifiers, vendor ID, account settings, profile information and information used to secure or manage access.
  • Vendor and listing details: business or store name, contact details, product or service descriptions, categories, prices, photographs and other materials submitted for publication.
  • Subscription and service records: vendor registration status, relevant payment confirmation or transaction references where a paid feature is used. Payments may be processed by payment service providers.
  • Technical and usage information: IP address, browser and device information, approximate location inferred from technical information, pages viewed, activity logs, cookie identifiers and basic diagnostic information.
  • Communications: requests, complaints, support messages, vendor reports and any other information you send to us.

Please avoid publishing personal information in listings that you do not want other people to see.

3. How and Why We Use Information

We process information as needed to:

  • Set up, authenticate, maintain and support user and vendor accounts.
  • Display store profiles, products and vendor contact details to enable genuine customer-vendor communications.
  • Administer subscriptions, confirm payments, provide purchased features and maintain business records.
  • Respond to inquiries, service requests, complaints and safety concerns.
  • Improve the website, diagnose faults, understand usage and protect platform security.
  • Detect or investigate scams, unauthorized data collection, misuse and violations of our Terms and Conditions.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements and communicate material service or policy changes.

Where required, we obtain consent for optional marketing, non-essential cookies or other processing that requires consent.

4. Lawful Bases for Processing

We rely on an appropriate lawful basis for each processing purpose under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable requirements. Depending on the activity, this may include performing a contract with you, complying with a legal obligation, pursuing a legitimate interest that does not override your rights, or obtaining your consent where required. Consent may be withdrawn as described below.

5. Vendor Information Visibility and Intended Use

Vendor store names, product listings and business contact details may be visible publicly to help buyers discover businesses and make legitimate inquiries. Vendors should provide business contact information that they are comfortable making public.

Public visibility does not mean that a vendor has agreed to receive unrelated commercial advertising, mass recruitment messages or third-party promotional campaigns. The permitted uses of information made accessible through BRYT DGM are also addressed in our Terms and Conditions.

Vendors remain independent businesses and may freely choose to work with other marketplaces or services.

6. Third-Party Collection, Marketing and Misuse of Vendor Contacts

BRYT DGM does not authorize visitors or third parties to systematically collect, extract or compile vendor contact details from our website for unsolicited advertising, bulk messaging, competing-platform recruitment or commercial contact databases, whether the collection is carried out manually or by automated means.

Third parties that independently collect or use personal information are responsible for identifying their own lawful basis, providing any required privacy information and respecting individuals' rights under applicable law. A third party's access to publicly displayed data does not automatically establish consent to direct marketing.

If you believe your contact details were obtained through BRYT DGM and misused, contact support@brytdgm.name.ng with relevant evidence. We may investigate and take reasonable action that is lawful and technically available to us. We cannot guarantee that all unauthorized copying or contact by third parties will be prevented.

7. When We Share Information

We do not sell users' personal information as a business practice. Information may nevertheless be available or shared in these circumstances:

  • With website visitors: publicly listed vendor information, including details a vendor chooses to display.
  • With service providers: hosting, security, communications, technical support, analytics or payment service providers acting to support the marketplace and subject to appropriate safeguards.
  • For legal and safety reasons: when disclosure is required by law or reasonably needed to investigate fraud, protect rights or respond to lawful requests.

We do not treat publication of a vendor contact number as permission for unrelated businesses to use that number for unsolicited promotional messaging.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies and similar technologies for basic functionality, security, user preferences and to understand website usage. Some cookies are necessary for website operation; other cookies may require your consent.

Where consent is legally required, we will seek it before using optional cookies or trackers. Browser settings may also allow you to block or delete cookies, although doing so can affect parts of the service.

9. Information Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps appropriate to the information we process, including access controls and measures intended to reduce unauthorized access, loss, disclosure or misuse. No website or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If a personal-data incident occurs, we will assess and respond to it in accordance with applicable legal obligations.

10. Storage, Retention and International Processing

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide accounts and platform services, maintain appropriate financial or legal records, resolve complaints and disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by category of information and purpose.

You may request deletion, subject to information we are permitted or required to retain. Where service providers process data outside Nigeria, we will apply safeguards and other requirements applicable to such processing under Nigerian data protection law.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and any valid legal exceptions, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal data we hold about you and information about how we process it.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request erasure or restriction of processing in applicable circumstances.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on consent, without affecting prior lawful processing.
  • Object to processing for direct marketing and object to certain other processing where permitted by law.
  • Request data portability where applicable, and lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To make a privacy request, contact support@brytdgm.name.ng. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting on a request.

12. Children and Young Users

BRYT DGM accounts are intended for people aged at least 16, subject to the eligibility requirements in our Terms and Conditions. Where a user is under 18 or otherwise lacks legal capacity to consent, any required consent from a parent or legal guardian must be obtained before processing that relies on such consent.

We do not knowingly permit accounts for people younger than 16. If we become aware that information was collected contrary to our eligibility rules or applicable law, we will take appropriate steps, including deletion where required.

13. External Websites

Links or contact buttons may take you to third-party websites, payment providers, messaging services or vendor channels. We do not control those parties' privacy practices. Please read their policies before providing information to them.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may amend this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our practices, services or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on our website with a revised date. We will communicate significant changes where required or reasonably appropriate. Changes take effect when posted or on a later effective date identified in the notice.
