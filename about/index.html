Who We Are

Previously known as AFIT Digital Market

BRYT DGM is a dynamic digital marketplace created to connect buyers and sellers. Our goal is simple — to give student vendors a scalable space to showcase their products and make it easier for customers to discover what they need, all in one place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to:

  • Empower small businesses and student entrepreneurs.
  • Simplify product discovery for buyers within the campus ecosystem.
  • Build a reliable and accessible digital marketplace.

We are committed to helping vendors grow while providing users with a smooth and efficient browsing experience.

What We Do

BRYT DGM provides a platform where:

  • Vendors can list and promote their products.
  • Customers can explore a variety of items easily.
  • Businesses can gain visibility without the stress of building a full website.

Why Choose Us

  • Affordable Access: A seamless space to start showcasing your products.
  • User-Friendly: Intuitive navigation for both vendors and buyers.
  • Community Built: A platform designed specifically to support local student businesses.
  • Targeted Visibility: Vendors get direct exposure to their target audience.

Our Vision

We aim to become the leading digital marketplace that supports innovation, entrepreneurship, and business growth, especially among students and young creators.
